# production

The work after the take: editing, mixing, and home-production technique. Comping,
timing and pitch correction, EQ, compression, effects, arrangement in the box, and
the long craft of making a mix sound like a record.

If recording is taking the photograph, production is the darkroom — you're not
changing what happened, you're deciding how it's seen. Suggested files:

- `mixing.md` — moves that worked: EQ, compression, balance, space.
- `editing.md` — comping, timing, tuning workflow.
- `daw.md` — templates, routing, shortcuts, plugin chains.
- `references.md` — mixes you're chasing and what specifically you want from each.

Capture the *why* behind a move, not just the setting. "High-passed the guitars at
120 to unclog the low end" is a lesson; "HPF 120" is a knob position you'll forget.
