# recording

Capturing sound — everything up to and including the take. Mic technique and
placement, signal chains, interface and DAW setup, gear notes, and session logs.

Recording is the moment the performance becomes a fact. Get it wrong here and no
amount of production downstream fully recovers it, so this drawer is worth real
attention. Suggested files as they earn their keep:

- `signal-chain.md` — what plugs into what, and why.
- `mic-technique.md` — placement notes per instrument and source.
- `gear.md` — the inventory and its quirks.
- `sessions/` — dated session notes: what was tracked, what worked, what to fix next time.

Keep the production work — mixing, editing — next door in [`production/`](../production).
The line: recording ends when the take is captured; production begins after.
