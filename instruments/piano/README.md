# piano

Standing reference for piano/keys. The instrument where theory is laid out in
front of you — every interval visible, every chord a shape you can see before you
hear it. That makes it the natural bench for working out harmony that then
transfers to the fretted instruments.

Drawers to fill:

- **Technique** — hand independence, scales, arpeggios, pedaling.
- **Harmony at the keys** — voicings, inversions, voice leading you can *see*.
- **Songs / pieces in progress**.
- **Goals**.
